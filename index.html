Core Study Features for Software Engineers

Spaced Repetition Study Tracker

Uses the SM-2 algorithm to calculate optimal review intervals based on your confidence. Study data structures, algorithms, and system design topics efficiently without over-studying what you already know.

Active Recall Question Bank

Practice flashcards covering essential technical questions. Test your knowledge, track your recall rate, and dynamically classify topics into weak, medium, and strong categories.

AI Mock Interviews (Spoken & Evaluated)

Speak directly into your mic to answer Gemini-powered technical and behavioral questions. Receive real-time speech transcription, pacing assessment, filler word counting, and depth feedback.

Daily Study Planner & Roadmaps

Set custom goals, plan revisions, log mistakes in a dedicated mistake pattern journal, and follow structured roadmaps designed for FAANG and high-growth startups.

Webcam Hand Gesture Controls

Experience hands-free navigation. Utilize computer vision (MediaPipe) via your webcam to swipe cards, scroll, and toggle settings using natural hand movements.

PWA Offline Mode & Sync

Fully mobile-responsive, works offline with local storage, and offers seamless cloud backup and sync through optional Firebase Authentication integrations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PrepFlow / Preparation Tracker really free?

Yes, PrepFlow is completely free and open-source under the Apache-2.0 license. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no ads. You can fork it, customize it, and deploy it yourself.

Do I need to create an account to use PrepFlow?

No. PrepFlow works entirely offline with local storage. Firebase authentication is optional and only required if you want to sync your study data across multiple devices.

How does the AI Mock Interview scoring work in PrepFlow?

It uses the Gemini API to generate tailored interview questions, records your spoken responses via your microphone, transcribes them, and evaluates your keyword accuracy, filler words (like 'um' and 'uh'), clarity, and depth in real-time.

What is PrepFlow's gesture control feature?

Using MediaPipe's real-time hand tracking via your webcam, you can navigate the PrepFlow workspace hands-free. You can swipe to change tabs/questions, pinch to click buttons, and point to scroll.

Can I use PrepFlow (Preparation Tracker) on my phone?

Yes. The app includes a Capacitor configuration for Android with native speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and push notifications. The standard web app is also fully mobile-responsive.

How does spaced repetition work in PrepFlow?

PrepFlow implements the SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm, which calculates optimal review intervals based on your self-reported confidence ratings. Difficult topics appear more frequently, while mastered topics are spaced further apart.
