Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 11, 2026

Your privacy is a cornerstone of how we design PrepFlow. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is stored, and what options you have regarding your study data.

1. What Information We Collect

We believe in **Data Minimization**.

  • Study Information: All content you write (notes, flashcard subjects, mistake items, STAR stories) is kept on your device. We do not copy this to our servers.
  • API Credentials: Configured AI API keys are stored only inside your browser's private local storage space. We do not collect or inspect these keys.
  • Firebase Authentication Details: If you choose to enable cross-device syncing, we utilize standard Firebase Auth (email, password, or Google login). Firebase processes this authentication request.

2. Cookies and Storage Files

PrepFlow does not set any advertising, tracking, or cross-site profiling cookies. We utilize standard Web Storage (LocalStorage and IndexedDB) strictly for functional application needs, including:

  • Saving your active color theme choice.
  • Caching study state lists to support fully offline operational capability.
  • Maintaining local application state flags.

3. Device Permissions (Camera & Microphone)

To enable features like hands-free gesture control and AI Mock Interview recording, PrepFlow requests browser permissions for your camera and microphone:

  • Webcam Data: Framerate feeds are processed locally on your machine via client-side libraries. No image or video frames are saved or transmitted off your device.
  • Microphone Data: Captured audio is converted to speech transcriptions locally or dispatched directly to AI provider endpoints. No raw audio clips are sent or stored on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

When utilizing the AI Mock Interview or coaching components, you connect to third-party providers (such as Google Generative AI, Cerebras, or Groq) using your own API credentials. All text inputs or transcriptions dispatched to these endpoints are governed by the respective privacy policies of those providers. We advise reviewing their terms of service to understand how they treat generative API inputs.

5. Your Control & Data Erasure

Since your database is under your control, you can wipe it instantly at any time. Simply use the "Clear All Local Data" button inside the app settings, or clear your browser's site cookies and storage cache.
